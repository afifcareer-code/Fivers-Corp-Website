About Fivers

Fivers Corp is an AI-native venture studio exploring, building, and operating digital products and real-world businesses.

The story

From office AI to the real world.

  1. 1
    Where it started

    Tinkering with AI

    Fivers began as curiosity: playing with AI and trying to turn everyday activities into products.

  2. 2
    What it became

    An AI office

    That grew into the idea of an AI office, a platform where any need, whether entertainment or development, can produce something. Fivers Digital Office is that platform and the main product.

  3. 3
    What comes from it

    Derivative products and real businesses

    Derivative products are developed from the platform, such as Veluna, Financial Tracker, LinguaFlow, and FiveClip. Alongside software, Fivers is rooted in family businesses in Brebes, Central Java.

Principles

How decisions get made.

01

Look before building.

Investigate the problem, the user, and the market before any significant resource is committed.

02

Decide on evidence.

Every opportunity ends in an explicit decision: build, iterate, experiment, partner, park, or kill.

03

Humans stay accountable.

AI agents research, build, and operate within defined permissions. Direction, capital, and high-risk decisions stay human.

04

Be honest about stage.

Live, building, and experiment mean what they say. Early-stage businesses are shown as early stage.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

What is Fivers Corp?

Fivers Corp is an AI-native venture studio that discovers, builds, and operates digital products, experiments, and real-world businesses.

What does Fivers build?

Its main product is Fivers Digital Office, an AI office in development. Around it are digital products such as Veluna and Financial Tracker (live), LinguaFlow (in development), and experiments such as FiveClip and Forex Agent.

How did Fivers start?

Fivers started from tinkering with AI and trying to turn everyday activities into products. That led to the idea of an AI office, Fivers Digital Office, where any need, entertainment or development, can produce something.

What is Fivers Digital Office?

The main product of Fivers: an AI office, currently in development, where AI agents support work and where derivative products are developed.

Where is Fivers based?

Fivers is based in Indonesia. Its real-world family businesses are in Brebes, Central Java.

What are Fivers' businesses?

Two early-stage family businesses in Brebes, Central Java: agriculture (onion farming, and occasionally rice) and layer chicken.

How does Fivers use AI?

AI agents support research, analysis, building, and operations within defined permissions. Humans set direction, make decisions, and approve high-risk actions.

What is an AI-native venture studio?

A company that designs its research, building, and operations around AI from the start, and uses that to explore and run several ventures instead of a single product.

What is Fivers Discovery?

The research and validation layer of Fivers, where ideas are investigated, assumptions are challenged, and decisions are made before significant resources are committed.

How does Fivers decide what to build?

Each opportunity moves through Discover, Validate, Experiment, and Decide. The decision can be to build, iterate, experiment further, partner, park, or kill.

Does every idea become a product?

No. Some ideas become products, some become experiments, and some should never be built. Discovery exists to find out which is which.
