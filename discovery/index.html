Research before building

Fivers Discovery

We don't build every idea.

Fivers Discovery is the research and validation layer of the company, where ideas are investigated, assumptions are challenged, opportunities are tested, and decisions are made before significant resources are committed.

Framework

Four stages, one decision.

01

DISCOVER

Understand the opportunity.

  • Problem
  • User
  • Jobs to be done
  • Market
  • Competitors
  • Opportunity
02

VALIDATE

Challenge assumptions through:

  • Research
  • Market signals
  • User feedback
  • Demand validation
  • Business model validation
03

EXPERIMENT

Build the smallest useful experiment, such as:

  • Prototype
  • MVP
  • Landing page
  • Internal tool
  • Technical proof of concept
04

DECIDE

Choose an outcome. Build is never the default.

  • Build
  • Iterate
  • Experiment
  • Partner
  • Park
  • Kill
Outcomes

Build is not the default.

Every opportunity ends in a decision. Any of these is a good result if the evidence points there.

  • BUILDCommit resources and build it.
  • ITERATEImprove the idea and test again.
  • EXPERIMENTRun another small experiment.
  • PARTNERWork with someone better placed to do it.
  • PARKNot now. Revisit when conditions change.
  • KILLStop. Learn from it and move on.
